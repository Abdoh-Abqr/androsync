AndroSync keeps folders you choose identical between your devices (currently Linux and Android), in either direction, over the USB cable or over the internet via Tailscale. Nothing is uploaded to any cloud. This app is the remote control for the AndroSync program on your Linux computer: you add folder pairs, preview what would change, start a sync, watch the live log, stop it, and undo a whole sync.

Why it is different:
• Preview first: see every copy and delete before anything happens.
• Undo: put both sides back to how they were before the last sync.
• Stop: a running sync can be stopped from the phone, the laptop window or the command line.
• Conflicts are not duplicated: the losing version is kept in a hidden folder, not as extra files in your folders.
• Safety: a first sync never deletes, and a sync that would delete or copy a lot waits for your OK.
• Works over the USB cable, over Wi-Fi debugging, or without any debugging in agent mode.

Agent mode (this version): the app reads and writes your folders itself when the laptop asks, over Tailscale, so USB debugging can stay off. It needs the "All files access" permission, which is why this version is not on Google Play.

You need the AndroSync program on a Linux computer: laptop, desktop PC or server (free, same project). Guide: github.com/Abdoh-Abqr/androsync

Free software, GPL-3.0-or-later. No ads, no tracking, no accounts. Nothing is sent anywhere except between your own phone and your own laptop.
